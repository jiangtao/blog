## Deliverables for the Next Generation of Product and Engineering

### 1. An evolvable specification

Evolving specifications are central to product-and-engineering collaboration. A good spec should provide:

- **Version control**: every iteration remains traceable.
- **Incremental updates**: requirements can change gradually instead of being rewritten wholesale.
- **Bidirectional alignment**: design and code stay in sync.

### 2. A product that can be accepted with confidence

Clear acceptance criteria determine delivery quality:

- **A clear definition of done**: measurable acceptance conditions.
- **Automated verification**: less subjective manual judgment.
- **Continuous integration safeguards**: every commit triggers verification through a complete, reliable pipeline.

### 3. Continuously observable acceptance criteria

Release is not the end; it is the beginning of observation:

- **Production metric tracking**: user behavior, performance, error rates, and alert feedback.
- **A/B experimentation**: validate improvements scientifically.
- **Feedback loops**: use observations to evolve the spec, and use feedback to improve both the spec and its standards.

## Supporting capabilities

### 1. A runtime environment that can keep evolving

> Open-ended, secure, and resilient.

- **Environment parity**: development, testing, and production remain aligned.
- **Resource isolation**: execute in sandboxes to prevent cross-impact.
- **Fast recovery**: rebuild or roll back quickly after a failure.
- **Open-ended execution**: maintain a clear context that automatically reduces noise and retains critical information so AI can continue operating.

### 2. People who can communicate the delivery chain clearly

> Product, engineering, and other collaborators.

- **Shared understanding**: everyone has the same view of the goal.
- **Transparent communication**: surface progress, risks, and changes promptly.
- **Clear responsibility boundaries**: define each role's ownership and authority.

## Before that future arrives

Until the ideal state is here, reshape and strengthen existing product-and-engineering workflows:

1. **Improve incrementally**: do not aim for a one-shot transformation; remove bottlenecks first.
2. **Let tools lead**: use automation to reduce human error.
3. **Build the culture**: establish a shared commitment to continuous improvement.
